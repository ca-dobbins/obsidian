### Manufacturing
*how to make the shtuff*
- Manufacturing Line
	- single product/product group
	- multiple stations but same product
	- item can be removed for rework or stay partially finished
	- automobiles, vacuum cleaner, washing machine, aircraft
- Continuous flow
	- inflexible (items can't be removed)
	- significant cost to change produced item
	- plastics, pharmaceuticals, chemicals
- Custom Manufacturing
	- single item or very low volume
	- wide range of products/customized
- Fixed Position Manufacturing
	- items don't move
	- can move after finished
	- ship building (old), conveyor belt, paint booths
- **Just-In-Time Manufacturing**
	- manufacturing that responds to demand
	- Takt time (rate of production)
***Kaizen Principle***: I can always make it better

| Pros                  | Cons                       |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Reduced costs         | Supply chain vulnerability |
| improved quality      | demand variability         |
| shorter lead times    | dependant on precision     |
| increased flexibility | employee training          |
| space savings         |                            |
- Lean Manufacturing
	- making obvious what adds value by reducing everything else
	- *Muri, Mura, Muda*
		- Muri: unreasonable work
		- Mura: irregularity
		- Muda: transport, inventory, motion, waiting, overproduction, overprocessing, defects
