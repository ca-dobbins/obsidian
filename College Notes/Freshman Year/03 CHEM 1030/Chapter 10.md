### Properties
***Pressure***
- Pressure is the force exerted per unit area by gas molecules as they strike the surfaces around them
- Depends on
	- number of gas particles in a given volume
	- volume of the container
	- average speed of the gas particles

***Basic Properties***
- Pressure (P)
- Volume (L)
- Temperature (T)
	- In Kelvin (C+273)
- Amount in moles (n)
### Laws
***Boyle's Law***
- relates pressure and volume
- volume and pressure are inversely related ($P \propto $)
