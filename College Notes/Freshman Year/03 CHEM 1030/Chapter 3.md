### History of the Periodic Table
*subtitle*
Mendeleev's Periodic Law (1896):
- when the elements are arranged in order of mass, certain sets of properties recur periodically

***Modern Periodic Table***
The elements are in order of increasing atomic #
Rows - periods
Columns - groups/families and have similar properties

*Groupings*
- metals
- non-metals
- metalloids
Can also be divided into:
- main-group elements:
	- predictable patterns/properties
	- **anything with a # + A on P.T**
- transition elements:
	- less predictable
	- **anything with a # + B on P.T**

### Electron Configs
*subtitle*
Schrodinger's eq for the hydrogen atom showed that its one electron will occupy the lowest energy orbital
$$
H \, -
\begin{align}
n=1 \\
l=0 \\
m_{l}=0 \\
m_{s}=+\frac{1}{2}
\end{align}
$$
two concepts affect multi-e atoms

**E Spin**
- all e have equal spin
	- up/down (eq and opp)
	- $+\frac{1}{2} \,\text{and} -\frac{1}{2}$
- half arrows (start with up)
- spins must cancel out
- $\ce{He - 1s^{2}}$
- e are paired, meaning diamagnetic
	*Paulie Exclusion Principle*:
	- no two numbers may have the same set of four QNs

**Sublevel Spliting**
- sublevels (QN l) all have the same energy
- orbitals w the same energy are said to be degenerate (equal)
	- for multi electron atoms, the energies of sublevels are split
		- charge int, shielding, penetration
- lower the value l, the less energy the sublevel has
![[Pasted image 20260908100127.png]]
*Electrons fill orbitals from lowest to highest - Aufbau Principle*
$\ce{1s^{n} 2s^{n} 2p^{n}\dots}$

***Coulomb's Law***
attractions and repulsions between charged particles
like charges - PE = + | decreases as they get farther apart
opp charges - PE = - | value becomes more negative as they get closer
*strength increases as size of the charges increases*

***Filling the Orbitals***:
when more e are added, each e experiences both attraction to protons and repulsion by other e (shielding: other e block/interfere w attractive forces)
- total amount of attraction that an e feels is called the effective nuclear charge     ($\ce{z_{eff}}$)
	***Hun's Rule***:
	- when filling orbitals that are degenerate, place one e in each orbital before completing pairs
