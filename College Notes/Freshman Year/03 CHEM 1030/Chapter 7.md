### Chemical Equations
- provide info about the reaction
	- can be$\ce{(g)}$,$\ce{(s)}$,$\ce{(l)}$,$\ce{(aq)}$.
- can be used to determine weights of reactants used and products that can be made
**Law of Conservation of Mass**: the sum of the masses of the reactants must equal the sum of the masses of the products

the study of the numerical relationship between chemical quantities is called *stoichiometry*
$$
\ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2H2O}
$$
##### Example(s):
Write a balanced equation for the reaction between solid cobalt(III) oxide $\ce{Co2O3}(s)$ and solid carbon $\ce{C}(s)$ that produces solid cobalt $\ce{Co}(s)$ and carbon dioxide gas.
$$
\ce{Co2O3}
$$
