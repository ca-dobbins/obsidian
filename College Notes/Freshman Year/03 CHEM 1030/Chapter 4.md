### Chemical Bonding
*subtitle*
2+ elements combine, a new substance forms
- The same: $\ce{O2}$
- Different: $\ce{H2O}$
***Two main types***:
- ionic
- covalent
- *metallic (not usually seen)*
![[Pasted image 20260922085340.png]]

#### Ionic Bonds
- involves the **transfer of electrons** between a **metal** and a **nonmetal**
	- metal -> cation
	- nonmetal -> anion
- composed of a lattice
#### Covalent Bonds (molecular)
- involve the **sharing of electrons** between two **nonmetals**
