### Solution Concentration
Homogeneous mixtures are called solutions -> two parts
- *Solute*: the thing that is dissolved
- *Solvent*: thing that is doing the dissolving

Dilute: small ratio of solute to solvent

Concentrated: large ratio of solute to solvent

A common way to express concentration is **Molarity** $M$:

$$
\text{Molarity}=\frac{\text{amount of solute (mol)}}{\text{volume of solution (L)}}
$$

This **Molarity** can be used as a conversion factor between moles of the solute and liters of the solution

Dilutions are when a new solution is prepared from a stock solution and is calculated using:

$$
M_{1}V_{1}=M_{2}V_{2}
$$
