### Lewis Structures
*recall: [[Chapter 4#Octet Rule|Octet Rule]]*
***Steps for drawing lewis structures***
1. Determine total valence $e^-$
	1. $\ce{CO2 ->16e^-}$
	2. *all atoms and charges*
2. Arrange atoms:
	1. generally less $e^-$-negative atom (except H) goes in the middle
3. Distribute $e^-$ among the atoms
4. If any atoms lack an octet, form double/triple bonds to form octets

#### Resonance
when 2+ valid lewis structures can be drawn for the same compound
- $e^-$ are delocalized from one atom
	- this helps to stabilize the molecule
- resonance structure
	- when there is more than one lewis structure for a molecule that differ only in $e^-$ position
	- resonance hybrid: combination of the resonance forms

#### Formal Charge
allows us to discriminate between lewis structures
***Rules***
1. the sum of all formal charges in a neutral molecule must be zero
2. the sum of all formal charges in an ion must = the ion
3. small/zero formal charges on individual atoms are better than large ones
4. negative formal charge must reside on the most electronegative

FC=(# of valence)-(# of nonbinding electrons)-(# of bonds)
