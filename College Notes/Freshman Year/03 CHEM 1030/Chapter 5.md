### Lewis Structures
*recall: [[Chapter 4#Octet Rule|Octet Rule]]*
***Steps for drawing lewis structures***
1. Determine total valence $e^-$
	1. $\ce{CO2 ->16e^-}$
	2. *all atoms and charges*
2. Arrange atoms:
	1. generally less $e^-$-negative atom (except H) goes in the middle
3. Distribute $e^-$ among the atoms
4. If any atoms lack an octet, form double/triple bonds to form octets

#### Resonance
when 2+ valid lewis structures can be drawn for the same compound
- $e^-$ are delocalized from one atom
	- this helps to stabilize the molecule
- resonance structure
	- when there is more than one lewis structure for a molecule that differ only in $e^-$ position
	- resonance hybrid: combination of the resonance forms

#### Formal Charge
allows us to discriminate between lewis structures
***Rules***
1. the sum of all formal charges in a neutral molecule must be zero
2. the sum of all formal charges in an ion must = the ion
3. small/zero formal charges on individual atoms are better than large ones
4. negative formal charge must reside on the most electronegative

FC=(# of valence)-(# of nonbinding electrons)-(# of bonds)

### Bonds
*subtitle*
#### Bond Energy
the amount of energy that it take to break on emole of a bond in a compound:
$$
\Delta H=\text{(bonds broken)}-\text{(bonds formed)}
$$
- the more electrons two atoms share, the stronger the bond
- the shorter the covalent bonds, the stronger the bond
![[Pasted image 20261006093821.png]]

#### Bond Length
the distance between the nuclei of bonded atoms
- triple < double < single

generally:
- bond length decreases from left to right 
- bond length increases down a column

#### VSEPR
electron groups around an atom are most stable when they are as far apart as possible

***Steric Number***
- bonds to central atom = 1 group
- lone pairs = 1 group
the steric number is used to find the arrangements of electron groups
- linear: SN 2
	- bond angle $180\degree$
- trigonal planar: SN 3
	- bond angle $120\degree$
- tetrahedral: SN 4
	- bond angle $109.5\degree$
- bipyramidal: SN 5
	- axial: on axis (top-bottom)
	- equatorial: in the same plane
		- angle between axial-eq $90\degree$
		- angle between eq-eq $120\degree$
- octahedral: SN 6
	- bond angle $90\degree$
