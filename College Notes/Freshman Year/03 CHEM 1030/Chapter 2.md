### Waves
*cool moves man*
wavelength and amplitude are independant properties

***wavelength ($\lambda$)***
- distance covered by a wave
- also determines color/type (intensive property)

***amplitude***
- strongness of wave (intensity)
- measured from midpoint to peak (height)
- determines brightness (extensive property)

light travelling at the same speed will have a shorter wavelength, so they pass more frequently
- wavelength $\lambda$ and frequency $\nu$ of electromagnetic waves are inversely proportional
$$
\begin{align}
\text{long} \,\lambda = \text{low} \, \nu \\ \\
\text{short} \, \lambda = \text{high} \, \nu \\
\end{align}
$$

$$
\nu=\frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

the interaction between waves is called _interference_
 - **constructive**
	 - add to make a larger wave, said to be *in phase*
 - **destructive**
	 - cancel each other out, said to be *out of phase*
**diffraction**
- when traveling waves encounter an opening in an object similar in size to the wavelength, it bends around the opening (traveling particles do not diffract)

**photoelectric effect** - electrons emitted when light is shined on a surface
$$
Energy=h\times \nu
$$
the energy of a photon of light is directly proportional to its frequnecy
$$
Energy=\frac{h\times c}{\lambda}
$$
**Planck's Constant**: $h=6.626\times10^{-34}J\times s$ 
Binding energy for the photon to escape: $\phi=hv_{0}$
