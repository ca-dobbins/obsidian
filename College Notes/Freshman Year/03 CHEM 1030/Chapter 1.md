### Matter
*what everything is made of*

Matter is composed of particles, and how they come together dictates its physicla properties. It is defined as anything that has mass and takes up space
- Atoms:
	- basic subatomic particle, fundamental building block
- Molecules:
	- particles formed from 2+ atoms
Matter is classified as:
- its state (physical form)
	- solid, liquid, gas
- its composition
	- types of molecules
*state of matter changes solid-liquid-gas as temp and pressure incrreases*
