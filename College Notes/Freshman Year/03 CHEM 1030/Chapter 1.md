### Matter
*what everything is made of*

Matter is composed of particles, and how they come together dictates its physical properties. It is defined as anything that has mass and takes up space
- Atoms:
	- basic subatomic particle, fundamental building block
- Molecules:
	- particles formed from 2+ atoms
Matter is classified as:
- its state (physical form)
	- solid, liquid, gas
- its composition
	- types of molecules
*state of matter changes solid-liquid-gas as temp and pressure increases*

Matter can also be classified as:
- pure substance
	- one singular component
		- element
			- cannot be broken down further
			- ex: $\ce{He or O2}$ 
		- compound
			- composed of  $\geq$ 2 elements in a fixed proportion
			- ex: $\ce{H2O , NaCl}$ 
- mixture: varies from one sample to another
	- heterogeneous
		- varies from one region to another
		- ex: concrete or soup
	- homogeneous
		- uniform throughout sample
		- ex: saltwater, sweet tea

### Modern Atomic Theory
*subtitle*
**Law of Conservation of Mass**
- matter is nether created or destroyed
**Law of Definite Proportions**
- All samples of a given compound, regardless of their source of how they were
prepared, have the same proportions of their constituent elements  ($\ce{H2O is always H2O}$)
**Law of Multiple Proportions**
- When two elements (call them A and B) form two different compounds, the
masses of element B the combine with 1 g of element A can be expressed as a
ratio of small whole numbers
