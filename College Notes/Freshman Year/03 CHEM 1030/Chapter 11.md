### Why are molecules attracted to each other?
- Intermolecular attractions are due to attractive forces between opposite charges.
	- (+) ion to (-) ion or cation to anion
	- (+) end of polar molecule to (-) end of polar molecule (dipole)
	- even nonpolar molecules will have temporary charges (instantaneous dipoles)
- **Larger charge = stronger attraction**
- **Longer distance = weaker attraction**
- *Inter*molecular forces are small relative to the bonding forces
