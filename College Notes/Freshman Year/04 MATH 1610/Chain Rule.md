### Idea of the Chain Rule
*subtitle*
how do we find $\frac{d}{dx}\sqrt{ x^{3}+x }$?
Let $f(x)=\sqrt{ x^{3}+x }$. It turns out $f(x)$ is a composition of two functions. So $f(u)=\sqrt{ u }$ and $u=x^{3}+x$; meaning $f(x)=f(u(x))$.
