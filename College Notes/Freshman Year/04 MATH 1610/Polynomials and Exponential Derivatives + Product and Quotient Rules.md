### 3.1
*subtitle*
Review: [[Derivatives as Functions#Taking the Derivative|Derivatives as Functions]]
***Power Rule***:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}=x^n=nx^{n-1}
$$
***Constants***:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}cf(x)=cf'(x)
$$
***Sum/Difference of two functions***:

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)+g(x)]=f'(x)+g'(x) \\
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)-(x)]=f'(x)-g'(x) 
\end{align}
$$
***Exponentials***:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}e^x= e^x
$$

##### Example(s):
A.
$$
\frac{d}{dx}12x^3=12\frac{d}{dx}x^3=36x^2  
$$
B.
$$
\frac{d}{dx} \frac{x^3+\sqrt[3]{ x^2}-x^e }{\sqrt[4]{ x }}=\frac{11}{4}x^{7/4}+\frac{5}{12}x^{-7/2}-\left( e-\frac{1}{4} \right)x^{e-5/4}
$$


### 3.2
*subtitle*
***Product Rule***:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)g(x)]=f(x)g'(x)+f'(x)g(x)
$$
***Quotient Rule***:

$$
\frac{d}{dx} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{g(x)f'(x)-f(x)g'(x)}{g(x)^2}
$$

##### Example(s):
A.
$$
\frac{d}{dx}x^2e^x=x^2\frac{d}{dx}e^x+e^x\frac{d}{dx}x^2=x^2e^x+2xe^x
$$
B.
$$
\frac{d}{dx} \frac{5e^x}{(x^{2}+1)(x^{3}+x-2)} 
$$
