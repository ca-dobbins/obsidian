### Rolle's Theorem
*subtitle*

***Def***: Let $f(x)$ be a function satisfying all of the following:
- $f(x)$ is continuous on $[a,b]$
- $f(x)$ is differentiable on $(a,b)$
- $f(a)=f(b)$
Then there exists a value $c$ in $(a,b)$ such that $f'(c)=0$.

### Mean Value Theorem (MVT)
*subtitle*

***Def***: Suppose $f$ is a function satisfying both:
- $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$
- $f$ is differentiable on $(a,b)$
Then there exists a $c$ in $(a,b)$ such that:
$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}
$$
