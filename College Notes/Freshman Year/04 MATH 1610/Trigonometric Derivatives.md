### Trig Limits
$$
\lim_{ \theta \to 0 } \frac{\sin \theta}{\theta}=1
$$

$$
\lim_{ h \to 0 } \frac{\cos(h) -1}{h}=0
$$
##### Example(s):
A.

$$
\lim_{ x \to 0 } \frac{\sin4x}{4x}=1
$$

B.

$$
\lim_{ x \to 0 } \frac{\sin7x}{3x}=\frac{7}{3}
$$

C.

$$
\lim_{ x \to 0 } \frac{\sin2x}{\sin5x}=\frac{2}{5}
$$

### Derivatives
$$
\large
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x  & \frac{d}{dx}\csc x=-\csc x\cot x\\
\frac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x  & \frac{d}{dx}=\sec x=\sec x\tan x\\
\frac{d}{dx}\tan x=\sec^2x & \frac{d}{dx}=\cot x=-\csc^2x
\end{array}
$$

$$

$$
