### Procedure
1. Read the problem
	1. what is being optimized?
	2. identify the constraint(s)
	3. what else is given?
2. Draw a labeled picture
3. Find an equation $Q$ that we want to optimize in terms of the labels
4. Use constraints (and other givens) to express $Q$ as a single variable function
5. Find the extrema of Q
	1. if the domain of $Q$ is a closed interval, use [[4.1#The Extreme Value Theorem (EVT)|The Extreme Value Theorem]]
	2. if the domain of $Q$ is not a closed interval, use [[4.3#1st Derivative Test|The First Derivative Test]]
##### Example(s):
A. A farmer has 2400 ft of fencing to enclose a rectangular pen bordering a straight river, needing no fencing on that side. What is the largest area a farmer can enclose?

$$
\begin{array}{}
\text{Area}= l\cdot w & 2400=l + 2w \\
\text{Area}=2400w-2w^2 & l=2400-2w 
\end{array}
$$
