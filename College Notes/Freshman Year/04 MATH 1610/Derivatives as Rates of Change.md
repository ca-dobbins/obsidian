### Tangent Line
*subtitle*

What is the tangent line to $x^{2}$ at $x=1$?
$$
m = \lim_{ x \to 1 } \frac{x^{2}-1}{x-1}=2
$$
***Def of Slope of the Curve***:
the tangent line to $f(x)$ at $x=a$ is the line through the point $(a,f(a))$ with slope,
$$
m=\lim_{ x \to a } \frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}
$$

***Def of the Derivative***:
The derivative of $f(x)$ at $x$ is:
$$
f'(x)=\lim_{ h \to 0 } \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$

##### Example(s):
A.
Let $f(x)=\frac{3}{x}$. Find the tangent line at $x=\frac{1}{2}$.
$$
ans(y) = m\left( x-\frac{1}{2} \right)+6
$$
$$
\begin{align}
f'(x)=-\frac{3}{x^{2}} \\
f'\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)=-12=m
\end{align}
$$
So,
$$
y=-12\left( x-\frac{1}{2} \right)+6
$$
