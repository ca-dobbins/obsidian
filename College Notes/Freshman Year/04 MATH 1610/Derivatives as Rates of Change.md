### Tangent Line
*subtitle*

What is the tangent line to $x^{2}$ at $x=1$?
$$
m = \lim_{ x \to 1 } \frac{x^{2}-1}{x-1}=2
$$
***Def of Slope of the Curve***:
the tangent line to $f(x)$ at $x=a$ is the line through the point $(a,f(a))$ with slope,
$$
m=\lim_{ x \to a } \frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}
$$

***Def of the Derivative***:
$$
\lim_{ h \to 0 } \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$
