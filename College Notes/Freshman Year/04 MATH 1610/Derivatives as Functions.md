### Taking the Derivative
*thank god*
Let $f(x)=\sqrt{ x }$:
$$
\lim_{ h \to 0 } \frac{\sqrt{ x+h }-\sqrt{ x }}{h} . \left( \frac{\sqrt{ x+h }+\sqrt{ x }}{\sqrt{ x+h }+\sqrt{ x }} \right)=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{ x }}
$$
So that means,
***Def***:
$$
f'(x)=\lim_{ h \to 0 } \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$
