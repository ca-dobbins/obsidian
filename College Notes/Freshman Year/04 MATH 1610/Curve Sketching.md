### Guidelines to Curve Sketching
1. Pre-calc stuff
	1. domain
	2. intercepts
		1. y-int: $f(0)$
		2. x-int: solve $f(x)=0$, only if practical
	3. symmetry
		1. even: y-axis: $f(-x)=f(x)$
		2. odd: origin: $f(-x)=-f(x)$
	4. periodicity: $f(x+p)=f(x),\;\text{where p is a constant}$
2. Asymptotes
	1. Horizontal: $\lim_{ x \to \infty }f(x)=L\;\text{and/or}\;\lim_{ x \to -\infty }f(x)=M$
	2. Vertical: $\lim_{ x \to a^+ }f(x)=\pm \infty$
